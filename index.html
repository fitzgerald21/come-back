Scene sounds and two-tone listening

Each scene can have its own sound: wind in the leaves, rain, waves. You can also add an exercise: two steady tones at once, like a fridge humming in two voices. Try to keep hearing both. When one slips away, or you lose both, tap, same as any other return. All of this can be changed during a sit, too.
